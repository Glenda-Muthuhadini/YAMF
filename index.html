Who We Are

Young Ambitious
Models Foundation
(YAMF)

A youth empowerment organisation dedicated to nurturing confident, purpose-driven young people through modelling, mentorship and personal development.

We create opportunities for young girls and boys to discover their potential, build confidence and develop the skills to become future leaders and positive changemakers in their communities.

Beauty With Purpose. Confidence With Character.

What We Do

At YAMF, we create opportunities for young people to grow in confidence, develop their talents
and become purpose-driven leaders through modelling, mentorship and community engagement.

Model Grooming &
Catwalk Training

Building confidence, presentation skills and professional runway techniques.

Pageant
Preparation

Preparing aspiring contestants with the skills, discipline and confidence to take the stage.

Mentorship &
Life Skills

Developing leadership, self-esteem, communication and personal growth beyond the runway.

Community
Outreach

Encouraging young people to give back through school visits, empowerment initiatives and community projects.
