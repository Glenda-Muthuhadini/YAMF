Who We Are

Young Ambitious Models Foundation (YAMF) is a non-profit organization (Reg No. 261-651 NPO) founded to uplift, train and mentor young aspiring models from disadvantaged backgrounds. We provide a platform for young girls and boys to discover their purpose, build self-confidence and use their beauty with brains to impact their communities.

Under YAMF, we have created several brands that work with partners around the world to help our models become more efficient and effective in pageantry. We have built relationships with brands outside South Africa 🇿🇦, including Botswana, Nigeria, Tanzania, Lesotho, Swaziland, Zimbabwe, Indonesia, the Philippines, Togo, Malawi and more. Through our brand Miss Teen Global Africa, African models meet and celebrate their unique cultures and diversity.

Our Mission

To discover, equip and empower young people with the confidence, skills and opportunities to reach their full potential.

Our Vision

A generation of confident, disciplined and empowered young people who lead, inspire and create positive change in their communities.

What We Do

We create platforms and programmes that develop young people holistically, including:

Modelling & Pageantry

Discovering and nurturing talent through modelling and purposeful pageants and competitions.

Youth Development

Empowering young people through personal growth, mentorship and life skills training.

Skills & Capacity Building

Equipping youth with practical skills, discipline and the confidence to succeed.

Leadership & Purpose

Instilling positive values, leadership and a strong sense of purpose to create impact.

Our Objectives
